# Grant Desk

Grant Desk is an Atlassian Forge app for Jira and Confluence. It helps small nonprofits find federal grants that fit, plan each application in Jira and draft sections from their own past proposals. A person approves every step.

- **Radar:** open opportunities from Grants.gov, scored against the organization's profile.
- **Fit check:** who may apply, time to prepare, topic, award size and cost sharing, plus the goals in Atlassian Home an opportunity supports.
- **Application plan:** nine steps scheduled back from the deadline, each with one owner, a person or the agent, and a Jira work item.
- **Answer library:** past proposals in Confluence, split into reusable sections.
- **Rovo agent and MCP tools:** draft sections with citations and marked gaps. Only a person approves, decides go or no-go, or submits.
